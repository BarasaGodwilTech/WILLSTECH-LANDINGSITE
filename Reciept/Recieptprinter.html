Receipt Number Format

Format: WTS-YYYYMMDD-HHMMSSmmmRR
Example: WTS-20241212-14304512345
Decodes to: 12/12/2024 at 14:30:45.123
YYYY = Year (2024)
MM = Month (12)
DD = Day (12)
HH = Hour (14 = 2 PM)
MM = Minute (30)
SS = Second (45)
mmm = Millisecond (123)
RR = Random digits (45)
