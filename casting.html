📺 Cast to Analog TV

Important: Your TV needs a WiFi receiver device connected via VGA. This page alone cannot cast directly to a VGA-only TV. See instructions below.

📋 Setup Required:

  1. For Raspberry Pi: Install a web browser in kiosk mode pointing to this page
  2. For Chromecast/Fire Stick: Cast your browser tab to the device
  3. Alternative: Use a spare smartphone/tablet connected to TV via HDMI-to-VGA adapter
